## 1. Summary

- **Visitors** now meet the chat as the Widget boards draw it, in the widget and on the hosted page: a glass header with the workspace's tile, name and a status line that says only what is true (the agent's name, or "Unavailable"); the 64 px orb above the greeting; the three voices; a typing pill with the breathing orb; a round glass composer; and, quietly at the foot, "Answers are generated by AI. · Powered by Nabra" (no link).

- **The workspace's colour** is used on the tile and Send only in a look (light or dark) where every contrast pair is proven. Otherwise emerald is used, and both the checks and the preview say so. The three voices never take the workspace's colour.
- **Channel screens** (hub, website chat list and link page, WhatsApp list and number) are calm panels. The corner is chosen from four radio cards, each with a small picture of the page. The install snippet is a code block with its Copy button.
- **Weight did not grow.** Startup JavaScript is 148,495 B (it was 148,525 B). The widget and the hosted page transfer about what they did at 39.5a and less than at 39.0, and their throttled-phone LCP is unchanged within run-to-run noise.

### 3.1 What changed, and why

- **The visitor's chat (`shared/chat/`)**, used by the widget, the hosted page and both previews:
  - **Header.** It is now a glass layer floating 12 px inside the panel. It holds the tile (the first character of the workspace's name, `aria-hidden`), the name (the page's only `h1`, in `bdi`) and the status line. New chat and Close use the shared `.btn` classes (secondary, and quiet icon).
  - **Transcript.** It scrolls under the header and fades out above it (a mask), so nothing shows in the 12 px band over the header.
  - **Opening.** The 64 px orb sits above the greeting (22/30, 600; Arabic 22/34). With no greeting, the orb sits above "Ask anything to start the conversation." until the first message.
  - **Voices.** The AI's bubble is always the AI voice (emerald) and no longer the workspace's tint. Visitor and team are as in 39.5a.
  - **Typing.** One pill in the AI's colour holds the three dots and "{agent} is typing…", beside the breathing orb.
  - **Composer.** A glass pill with a round 44 px Send. Its arrow mirrors in RTL, and its name stays in words ("Send", "Sending…") for screen readers and the e2e locators.
  - **Foot.** The handover notice (person glyph and sentence, in the team's gold), the alert, and the colophon: the honesty line plus "Powered by Nabra" / «بدعم من نبرة», with no link.
- **The colour rule (`embed/accent-color.ts`):**
  - `checkChatBrand(value, theme)`, `chatBrand(value)` and `applyChatBrand(element, brand)` are new.
  - The colour stands in for emerald in one look only if two things hold: a label in the near-black or the white ink reaches 4.5:1 (never the last-resort black), and the colour reaches 3:1 against both grounds of that look (light: cream-50 and white; dark: emerald-950 and emerald-900).
  - Pages set `--chat-brand-light` / `--chat-brand-dark` (and the `--chat-on-brand-*` ink) only where the colour holds. The token layer resolves `--chat-brand` per look on `.chat-panel`, else `--color-primary`.
  - A spec reads `_tokens.scss` and fails if the fixed grounds drift from the tokens.
  - The launcher keeps the widget's existing rules: the accent, with an outline when it is faint on white.
- **Public reads:** `HostedChatApi.config(key, lang)` and `EmbedChatApi.config(key, origin, lang)` send `?lang=en|ar`, the page's or the frame's language (`APP_LANGUAGE`). The greeting comes back in that language when the workspace wrote one.
- **Website chat settings (`website-chat-widget`):**
  - **Greetings.** Three boxes: English (`dir="ltr"`, `lang="en"`), Arabic (`dir="rtl"`, `lang="ar"`) and the default. Each is checked against 300 characters on the client. `greetings: { en, ar }` is always sent, and an empty box becomes `null`. The preview shows the greeting in the reader's language, else the default.
  - **Refused greetings.** A greeting refused by the server is worded by its `field` (`ApiError.field` and `ProblemFacts.field` are new). The words appear under that box, with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, and focus moves there.
  - **Corners.** Four `app-radio` cards in `fieldset#widget-position`, each with a small picture of where the launcher sits on a page in the reader's language. The selected card is tinted and edged.
  - **Colour checks.** Two lines say, for each look a visitor can see (System gives both looks, Light or Dark gives one), whether the tile and Send use the colour or Nabra's emerald, with the ratio. Under the preview, a note names the look where emerald is used.
  - **Snippet.** A code block with a bar ("HTML", "Copied.", Copy snippet).
  - **Swatch.** The native colour swatch is now the size of a control.
- **Channel screens** carry `.page-calm`: panels with radius 24, lists as one panel of rows, status words as chips. On the hub, the channel glyphs are soft tiles and "Not available yet" is one panel of rows.
- **Hosted page.** The visitor ground with the two glows (`--chat-glows`, a new token). The panel is a full-height column of at most 46 rem. The Nabra mark has left the header: the workspace's tile is there now, and "Powered by Nabra" names Nabra.
- **Widget frame.** The panel is radius 24 with a strong line, and opens on the dialog motion tokens.
- **Agent preview (39.5a)** uses the same colour rule and shows the greeting in the reader's language.
- **Tokens (`_tokens.scss`):** `--orb-ring`, `--text-greeting` (en and ar), `--chat-glows`, and the per-look `--chat-brand` / `--chat-on-brand` on `.chat-panel` (the dark half sits in `dark-palette`).

## 4. Decisions

1. **The workspace's colour replaces emerald only on the tile and Send, and per look.** The board's emerald is the primary role there. The voices stay emerald, gold and cream on every screen (spec §1: "the same three colours mean the same three voices … in the widget"). This reverses 39.5a's "AI bubble keeps the accent tint".
   - "Every contrast pair" is defined in `checkChatBrand`. A navy (`#1d3557`, Northwind's link) is used in light and falls back to emerald in dark (1.39:1). A pale yellow is the reverse. The old default blue `#2563eb` holds in light only (2.93:1 against the dark panel).
   - Each look is decided on its own, so a "System" widget can wear the colour in light and emerald in dark. The checks and the preview note say exactly that.
2. **The status line** says the agent's name with a small dot, or "Unavailable" with a hollow ring and the word. There is no line when the heading already is the agent's name (the agent preview). The board's "Replies in seconds" and its quick-reply chips are not offered by the API, so neither ships (BA39-4).
3. **The honesty line moved from the header to the foot,** beside "Powered by Nabra". The board's header has two lines (name and status), and the honesty line must stay on every customer surface. The e2e check on `.chat-honesty` still passes.
4. **Send is a round icon button** whose words are visually hidden. Its accessible name and `textContent` are still "Send" / «إرسال», so `hosted-page-ar` passes without a change.
5. **The typing pill** keeps the existing "{agent} is typing…" rather than the board's bare "Typing…", which is less specific. No new string was needed.
6. **The corner picker is `app-radio` cards** (plan §3.7), so one e2e journey changed: `widget-corners.e2e.ts` uses `#widget-position` (now the fieldset), `getByRole('radio')` count 4 and `input[value=…].check()`, instead of `option` and `selectOption`. Its assertions are unchanged. The theme stays a select, because its first option, "Match the visitor's device", is too long for a segmented control.
7. **The Arabic word for greeting is «الترحيب»,** the word of the owner's sentence («يظهر هذا الترحيب للزوار الذين لا يوجد ترحيب بلغتهم.»), and no longer «التحية». The legend, its hint and the two greeting refusals were reworded so that one form uses one word (listed in section 8).
8. **The `field` of a refusal.** It became a top-level `ApiError.field` and `ProblemFacts.field`, a string only, so the shared catalogue words the greeting refusals: "The English greeting …", "The Arabic greeting …", "The default greeting …", or today's "This greeting …" when no field is named. Cost: a few bytes of startup JavaScript (it ended 30 B under HEAD after other moves).
9. **The glass budget inside the admin shell.** A panel drawn inside another page (the settings preview, the agent preview: `titleIsHeading=false`) keeps the glass look but drops the blur below 992 px. There the shell's two bars already blur, so the count stays at three or fewer (it was four before this fix).
10. **The 4 kB component-style budget, kept.** The per-look colour resolution and the glows moved into the token layer, the head buttons use the global `.btn` classes, the separator dot is in the template, and the colour swatch is a rounded square rather than a circle (no `::-webkit-color-swatch` rules). Final sizes: `chat-panel.scss` 3.78 kB and `website-chat-widget.scss` 3.94 kB. No budget was raised.
11. **The Nabra mark left the hosted page's header.** It now shows the workspace's tile, as the boards do. Nabra is named in the colophon instead. This departs from 38.3, which drew the mark when no accent was usable.
12. **The glows sit in physical corners** in both languages (emerald top-left, gold bottom-right), as both boards draw them.
13. **The frame sends its own language** (`APP_LANGUAGE`, which the loader's `?lang` sets when the host page declares en or ar). The greeting therefore matches the language the widget is written in.
14. **The «الأصوات مقفلة» note (member block, Arabic).** The catalogue contains no «مقفلة» at all. In the screenshot, sounds were on: the key shown is `account.soundsOn`, «الأصوات مفعّلة», and at 13 px on a 1× display the «عّ» with its shadda reads like «ق». With sounds off the line is `account.soundsOff`, «الأصوات معطّلة», as 39.3's table says. No key is wrong, so nothing was changed. The 3× capture shows the word clearly (`sidebar-sounds-on-1440-ar-dark-dpr3.png`). A legibility option for the owner is in section 9.
15. **Deferred:** nothing from this step's item list.

## 9. Risks and open issues

1. **The 39.5a decision on the AI bubble is reversed.** The AI bubble no longer takes a tint of the workspace's colour, so voices read the same everywhere. A workspace that liked its tinted replies will see emerald.
2. **The default blue holds in light only.** Workspaces still on the server's default `#2563eb` see emerald on Send and the tile in the dark look. This is intended (it falls under 3:1 against the dark panel), and the settings say so.
3. **«الأصوات مفعّلة» at 13 px.** It can be misread as «الأصوات مقفلة» (locked) on a 1× display. Options for the owner: 14 px for that line in Arabic, or a plainer pair such as «الأصوات تعمل» / «الأصوات متوقفة». Nothing was changed here, because the key and the wording are the accepted ones.
4. **The fading mask on the transcript.** `mask-image` is Baseline. A browser without it shows the old look (text passing under the glass header).
5. **Still open from 39.5a:** the phone bottom bar breaks "Knowledge" / "Customers" / "Overview" mid-word at 320–390 px (39.8b).
6. **Visitor conversations left on the dev data:** the checks left 4 conversations on Northwind's "Northwind Concierge" link, all returned to the AI, with the marker "P39.5b" in the person's reply.
