## 0. Name

- **Nabra** in English, **نبرة** in Arabic. The only name people who use the product ever see or
  hear: the wordmark, tab titles, sentences, accessible names, the loading state.
- Meaning: نبرة is "tone of voice". Every conversation has two voices, the AI and the team; Nabra is
  the one tone they speak in. The name says what the mark draws.
- Arabic form: نبرة, written in Arabic in Arabic copy and never transliterated there. Inside a
  sentence it takes the grammar of a feminine noun ("نبرة لا تستجيب", "الانتقال إلى نبرة",
  "تسجيل الدخول إلى نبرة"). In English copy it is "Nabra", never "NABRA" or "nabra".
- Tagline: "Two voices. One tone." / «صوتان… بنبرة واحدة».
- Wordmark: "Nabra" in Instrument Sans, narrowed like every title (`--heading-stretch`, tight
  tracking); "نبرة" in IBM Plex Sans Arabic at full width with no tracking (the same tokens under
  `:lang(ar)`). Always beside the mark, in the page's language: sidebar, sign-in, registration,
  invitation, workspace chooser, loading state. No other font files.
- Tab titles: "<page> · Nabra" and "<page> · نبرة" (`AppTitleStrategy`).
- **"OmniAI" is never shown to users.** It is the internal code name only: the repository,
  namespaces, folders, CSS classes, `data-testid`s, i18n message IDs, `localStorage` and cookie
  keys, protocol strings and headers, the database, Docker and environment variables. Copy
  always says Nabra or نبرة; the source of the name in code is `src/app/core/brand/brand.ts`
  (message `@@brand.name`).

## 1. Product and audience

- Product type: multi-tenant admin for AI customer conversations (website chat, WhatsApp,
  Instagram, Messenger), plus the customer-facing hosted chat and embeddable widget.
- Audience: workspace owners setting up AI agents; support staff and supervisors who watch the
  inbox and take conversations over during long shifts; analysts reading reports.
- Job to be done: "See who is answering my customers right now, the AI or one of us, and step
  in the moment a person is needed."
- Languages and directions: English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL, from Phase 36.3). Every screen is
  designed for both.
- Primary devices: desktop at 1280-1600 px for the admin; phones at 360-430 px for supervisors
  on the move and for every customer-facing surface.
- Brand attributes: calm, precise, trustworthy, quietly premium, legible for eight hours.
- Not this: playful, "magical AI", neon tech, decorative, editorial-retro.

## 2. Design direction

- Concept: every Nabra conversation is a duet between the AI and the team. The interface gives
  each voice one colour and shows the handoff between them everywhere. Colour is information,
  not decoration; everything else is calm ink on porcelain.
- Principles:
  1. The two voice colours mean only "the AI" (Lagoon) and "a person on the team" (Ember).
     Never decorative, never status.
  2. Ink is the action colour: primary buttons are ink on light and porcelain on dark. Links are
     ink and always underlined.
  3. Calm surfaces, crisp hairlines, depth only for overlays.
  4. Type with character: Instrument Sans narrowed for headings, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for
     Arabic, tabular figures for numbers.
  5. The handoff is the hero moment: chip, track, split bar and the "Take over" transition.
  6. Real data only. If the API cannot tell it, the design does not show it.
- Alternatives considered (Phase 36 direction study): the generic indigo admin it replaces
  (rejected: the AI-default look, and colour said nothing), and the other directions in the
  reference pack.

### 3.1 Glass, transparency and the other axes

- **Glass only where things float:** the side bar, the phone's top and bottom bars, menus and
  popovers, dialogs and the command palette, toasts, and the widget's header and composer.
  Conversations, tables, forms and charts stay solid. The `glass` mixin and the `.glass` class
  (`_glass.scss`) are the only places that declare `backdrop-filter`. Budget: at most three
  blurred layers on screen, never on a scrolling row, `will-change` only while animating.
- **Depth:** solid panels have a 1 px line and no shadow; only glass casts a shadow.
- **Axes, one switch each:**

  | Axis           | Switch                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Effect                                                                                   |
  | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Theme          | `data-theme="light"`/`"dark"` on `<html>` (stamped before first paint)                                                                                                                                                | the palette                                                                              |
  | Transparency   | `data-transparency="reduced"` (the account menu's "Reduce transparency", kept per browser in `omniai.transparency`, stamped before first paint on admin pages) **or** `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` | every glass becomes its solid twin: panel colour, the same border, no blur               |
  | Forced colours | `@media (forced-colors: active)`                                                                                                                                                                                      | system colours (`Canvas`, `CanvasText`, `Highlight`), no shadows, no blur, outline focus |
  | Contrast       | `@media (prefers-contrast: more)`                                                                                                                                                                                     | quiet text becomes secondary, lines become control borders                               |
  | Motion         | `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`                                                                                                                                                                             | durations to ~0 and travel to 0; fades keep `--duration-fade`                            |
  | Script         | `:lang(ar)`                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Plex Arabic, one more step of line height, letter-spacing 0                              |

  The visitor pages (`/chat/`, `/embed/`) follow the visitor's device for theme and
  transparency, never a choice stored by an admin in the same browser.

## 4. Typography

Self-hosted from `frontend/public/fonts/` with the OFL texts beside the files (Fontsource 5.3.0
builds); no font service is ever contacted (CSP `font-src 'self'`). Versioned, unhashed names
(`geist-latin.v1.woff2`): the build copies `public/` as it is and leaves the root-relative
`url('/fonts/…')` alone (verified in the 39.2a build output), so the pre-paint script can
preload exactly the URL the stylesheet uses. The edge caches `.woff2` for a year as immutable:
a changed file is `v2`.

- **Files** (bytes): Geist variable 100–900, Latin 29,400 and Latin Extended 16,512; IBM Plex
  Sans Arabic 400 / 500 / 600 / 700, Arabic subset only, 42,848 / 45,296 / 45,688 / 44,280;
  Instrument Serif 400, Latin 21,032 and Latin Extended 11,604. 700 is for Arabic display only;
  no running text uses a weight above 600 (the 650s of Two Voices became 600, so an Arabic page
  never fetches 700 for its lockup; 39.2b draws the lockup as outlined SVG).
- **Preload, per language**, from the pre-paint script in `index.html`: Geist Latin on every
  page (the Latin text and the digits of both languages); Arabic 400 on Arabic pages; and on
  English pages under `/conversations`, where customers often write in Arabic, Arabic 400 at
  `fetchpriority="low"`. Nothing else: Latin Extended, 500, 600, 700 and the serif load when a
  glyph needs them. Measured in 39.2a (Phase 39.2a report, section 5).
- **Metric-matched fallbacks** (measured in Chrome, `p39-2a/chrome/font-metrics.json`):
  `Geist Fallback` (Arial / Arimo / Liberation Sans / Helvetica; size-adjust 101.72%, ascent
  98.8%, descent 29%), `Instrument Serif Fallback` (Georgia / Times New Roman / Tinos; 76.14%,
  130.03%, 40.72%), `IBM Plex Sans Arabic Fallback` (Segoe UI / Tahoma / Geeza Pro / Noto Sans
  Arabic; 89.52%, 121.2%, 46.36%). The same paragraphs wrap to the same lines and heights in
  each pair. `font-display: swap`.
- Numerals policy: Western digits (0-9) in both languages, tabular in tables, metrics and
  meters, formatted with an explicit `numberingSystem: 'latn'` and a Gregorian calendar.
- Arabic rules (`:lang(ar)`): letter-spacing 0, no uppercase, no italics; one more step of line
  height than Latin; never under 13 px for running text; display in Plex Arabic 600–700 (the
  serif is Latin only). Mixed-direction strings use `dir="auto"` or `<bdi>`; emails, URLs,
  codes and IDs are isolated `dir="ltr"`.

## 5. Layout, spacing and density

- Spacing: 4 px scale, `--space-1…10` = 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56, 64 (Calm Glass: 48
  became 56). Nothing in between. Beside the scale, `--gap-hairline` (2 px) for things that read
  as one. The shell's rhythm: `--shell-padding` 14 px around the shell, `--column-gap` 12 px
  between columns, `--panel-padding` 24 px inside panels (39.3 onward).
- Density: controls 32 / 36 / 40 px (`--control-height-sm`, default, `-lg`), raised to 40-44 px
  under `pointer: coarse`, until 39.2b restyles buttons and inputs to Calm Glass's 44 px
  (`--control-height-dense` 40 px in dense desktop headers). `--target-min` 44 px for every touch
  target, the label included. Targets never under 24 px.
- Page frame: content capped at 1600 px; gutter 24 px, 16 px under 576 px.
- Patterns: page header + sections (not card soup); `.data-list` for record lists whose rows
  are links; `.table` for real tabular data; one pager pattern with the page in the URL.

## 6. Shape, depth, imagery and icons

- Radius by role (Calm Glass): `--radius-pill` 999 (buttons, chips, segmented tracks, the
  widget's input), `--radius-input` 12 (inputs, small cards; the old `--radius-control`),
  `--radius-nav-item` 12, `--radius-tile` 16 (rows and tiles; the old `--radius-card`),
  `--radius-panel` 24, `--radius-dialog` 24 (the old `--radius-overlay`), `--radius-bubble` 18
  with a 6 px "tail" corner on the speaker's side (logical corners, so it mirrors in RTL).
  `--radius-badge` 6 stays for checkboxes and small marks until 39.2b's choice controls.
- Nested radius: inner = outer − padding.
- Icons: one authored 24 px outline set (`app-icon`), stroke 1.6 with round caps and joins.
  Voice glyphs `ai` (a calm bot face, never a sparkle) and `person`. Channel glyphs are generic
  (`website`, `whatsapp` as a phone in a bubble, `instagram` as a camera, `messenger` as two
  bubbles) and always shown with the channel's name; no recoloured brand logos. Only directional
  icons mirror in RTL; the mark never does.
- Imagery: none in the admin. No illustrations in empty states.

## 7. Motion

- Stance: motion explains a change and then stops (calm-glass.md §5).
- Durations: `--duration-press` 120 ms (hover and press), `--duration-menu` 200 ms (menus,
  popovers, toasts), `--duration-dialog` 280 ms (dialogs and panels); `--duration-fade` 200 ms
  for a fade that stays under reduced motion.
- Easing: one curve, `--ease-calm` `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`.
- Travel: `--motion-travel` (1, or 0 under reduced motion) multiplies every distance; a new
  message rises `--motion-rise` (8 px); the orb breathes to `--orb-breath` (1.04) while the AI
  answers, still under reduced motion.
- The Two Voices names (`--duration-xs…xl`, `--ease-standard/-enter/-exit/-emphasized`,
  `--motion-fast/-normal/-slow`) are aliases of these until 39.8b.
- Not in the admin: count-ups, stagger on every visit, parallax, fade-up on sections, bounce.
- Reduced motion: the press, menu and dialog durations collapse to ~0 at the source and travel
  to 0, so every component follows; the base layer's universal rule stops literal infinite
  animations; a fade that must stay declares `--duration-fade` on its own rule. View
  Transitions stop. State changes stay visible.

## 8. Content and voice

- Plain, specific, calm. Say what happened and what is safe ("Nothing was lost").
- Buttons are verb + object ("Save changes", "Return to AI"); Arabic buttons prefer verbal
  nouns (حفظ, إرسال).
- The voices are named "AI" and "Person"; in Arabic الذكاء الاصطناعي and الفريق (approved
  36.3a). Arabic terms follow `frontend/src/locale/GLOSSARY.md`.
- Empty states say what belongs here and offer one next action.
- The customer-facing honesty line stays: answers are generated by AI.

## 9. Accessibility and RTL commitments

- WCAG 2.2 AA: text 4.5:1 (large 3:1), UI parts 3:1, proven per pair in both themes; targets at
  least 24 px (44 px on touch); visible focus (2 px ink ring, 2 px offset) never hidden; no
  colour-only meaning; reduced motion honoured; forced colours supported (outline focus, the
  mark and voice bars fall back to system colours).
- `lang` and `dir` on `html` per locale, set before first paint; logical CSS only; transforms
  that must mirror use `--direction-flip`; directional icons mirror through `MIRRORED_ICONS`
  (never at the use site); user content `dir="auto"` or `<bdi>`; emails, URLs, codes, IDs and
  numeric ranges are `dir="ltr"` islands. Component styles reach `[dir]` only through
  `:host-context([dir='rtl'])` (a plain `[dir='rtl']` selector never matches under emulated
  encapsulation). Full rules: `docs/architecture/localisation-arabic-rtl.md`.

## 10. Rules this project enforces

1. Emerald means the AI, gold the team, cream the customer, on every screen, in the widget, in
   emails and in report files. A voice is never decoration.
2. Emerald is the action colour. One primary action per area.
3. Links are emerald and underlined.
4. The selected row is a faint primary tint (`--color-selected`); the current nav item and the
   palette's highlighted option keep their 2 px edge (`.edge-marker`).
5. Shadows only on glass; glass only on the floating layers of section 3.1, with its solid twin
   under reduced transparency and forced colours.
6. Every status carries its own icon and word; every voice its glyph and word.
7. Voice displays (chip, split, track, divider) are drawn only from real API data.
8. No CDN, font service or third-party request from the application.
9. Components use semantic tokens only; shared rules go to the global layers, not budgets.
10. Sample content exists only in the development-only Design Lab, labelled as sample. One
    page exception: the labelled example conversation on the entry pages (`pages/entry.md`).

## 11. Avoid in this project (as of 2026-10)

Purple or indigo; decorative gradients beyond the two background glows and the orb; glass on
anything that is not floating, or on a scrolling row; more than three blurred layers; sparkle
icons for "AI"; emoji icons; Inter by default; same-size card mosaics with no purpose; the serif
in tables or forms, or in Arabic; terracotta; near-black + acid accent; fade-up on every
section; count-ups; stagger on every visit; cursor effects; splash screens; parallax;
recoloured brand logos; status shown by colour alone; a gold status chip beside a gold voice
chip (owner decision Q4).
